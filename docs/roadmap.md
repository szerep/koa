## Future / Deferred

Agreed ideas for after the current roadmap. **All of them are deferred until both Phase 9 and Phase 10 are complete and approved** (exception, by decision on 2026-11-09: item 1 became Phase 11 while Phase 9 stays open for its iPhone check). None is part of any current phase, and no implementation has started. Each one gets its own plan and approval first, in this priority order. Koa stays small, personal, free and simple: none of these assumes paid services or heavy infrastructure.

1. **Accessory artwork:** create the artwork and assets for the shop's accessories and integrate them into the app, through the gated art pipeline (character bible §3; required files in `docs/phase-6/accessory-assets.md`). The layering system already exists. **Started as Phase 11 (2026-11-09), with the beanie and round glasses only;** the other accessories stay here until approved.
2. **Wardrobe room:** a proper wardrobe room where owned accessories are displayed or hung and can be equipped. **Delivered as Phase 12 (complete 2026-11-18) as a Koa-centered Wardrobe screen,** without an illustrated room: displaying items in an illustrated room (hooks, shelves, furniture) and room theming stay deferred.
3. **Animations and personality expansion:** refine and expand Koa's animations and reactions, using approved artwork only (missing poses are never faked).
4. **Little Koala companion:** a small koala companion, bought in the Shop, that appears beside Koa. New character art goes through the same gated pipeline and must match Koa's style.
5. **Friends and shared progress:** invited friends can see each other's focus progress and streaks. Koa has no server or accounts today, so this needs its own design decision on the simplest free way to share progress.
